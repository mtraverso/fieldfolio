FieldFolio Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

FieldFolio is a local-first job book for solo service workers. Your clients, jobs, photos, estimates, and invoices stay on your device.

Data we collect

None. FieldFolio does not require an account and does not operate a FieldFolio backend for your content. We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports through third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

  • Business profile (name, phone, email, currency, payment instructions)
  • Clients, jobs, line items, notes, tax/discount amounts, and payment status
  • Before/after photos you capture or attach
  • Generated estimate and invoice PDFs you choose to share

Optional Face ID / Touch ID only unlocks the app on your device. Biometric data never leaves Apple’s secure enclave and is not accessible to FieldFolio.

Sharing

When you use the iOS share sheet to send a PDF, that content is delivered through the apps you choose (Messages, Mail, Files, etc.). FieldFolio does not upload that content to FieldFolio servers.

Backups

If you use Export backup, FieldFolio builds a ZIP file (CSV records and job photos) on your device. That file stays local until you share it yourself. FieldFolio does not upload backups to FieldFolio servers.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Apple may collect payment information under Apple’s privacy policy. FieldFolio only receives an entitlement signal that Pro is unlocked.

App Tracking Transparency

FieldFolio does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

FieldFolio is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
