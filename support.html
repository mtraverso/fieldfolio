Help for the FieldFolio iPhone app

What FieldFolio does

FieldFolio is a job book for solo cleaners, detailers, handymen, and similar operators. Track clients and jobs, capture before/after photos, send estimate and invoice PDFs, and mark jobs paid. No account required.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone. FieldFolio does not sync to a FieldFolio cloud.

How do I remove the PDF watermark? Upgrade to FieldFolio Pro in Settings.

Can FieldFolio take card payments? No. Clients pay you however you already get paid (Venmo, cash, transfer). FieldFolio records status only.

Is this tax or legal advice? No. Invoices are records for your own bookkeeping.

How do I restore a purchase? Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
